Ask ChatGPT for Moodle progress and grade insights

Read permitted course data and turn it into useful insights for learning support.

Copy ready-to-use prompt ↓How it works ↓
Real-world situation

An academic coordinator needs a view of class progress and activities that may be blocking completion.

Before you start

Connect Moodle to ChatGPT or Claude with appropriate permissions. To create or change data, the connection needs WRITE access and the signed-in user must have the relevant Moodle capabilities. Connection guide →

Step by step

  1. Identify the course and retrieve completion criteria and gradebook structure using authorized access.
  2. Request paginated course progress and grades allowed for your account.
  3. Summarize patterns without inventing learner identities or fields not returned by tools.
  4. Verify any critical finding inside Moodle before making decisions about learners.

Ready-to-use ChatGPT or Claude prompt

Copy the prompt, edit course names and attach relevant files. The assistant is asked to show its plan before writing to Moodle.

Example prompt
For the Moodle course I select, summarize course completion rules, progress distribution, activities that appear hardest to complete, and grades I am allowed to see. Use get_course_progress_report and get_grades as permitted, respecting pagination and separate-group boundaries. Do not infer learner identities from aggregate results or invent missing data. Explain your data sources and suggest pedagogical actions without modifying Moodle.

Tools used in this workflow

ChatGPT or Claude selects the tools exposed by the connected installation. These tool names exist in the plugin and are relevant to this example:

get_course_progress_reportget_course_completion_statusget_gradesget_course_completion_criteriaget_gradebook_structure

View tool reference and parameters →

How to verify the result

Reconcile totals, filters and pagination with Moodle. Distinguish activity-progress percentage from formal course completion.

Limitations and safeguards

The course progress report returns at most 25 records per page and does not include names, email addresses or individual grades. Individual queries depend on Moodle capabilities and group rules.

Protected WRITE operations show a preview and execute only after explicit confirmation. If the assistant cannot access a tool, it must report that limitation instead of claiming it published content.
