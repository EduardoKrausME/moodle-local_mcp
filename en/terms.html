1. Scope

Moodle MCP (local_mcp) is an independently maintained, open-source integration that lets authorized Model Context Protocol (MCP) clients interact with tools exposed by a Moodle installation. These terms address the project website, documentation, and related public project resources. They do not replace agreements or policies applicable to your institution's Moodle instance, ChatGPT, OpenAI, or any other client.

3. Authorized use

Only connect an MCP client to a Moodle installation when you have permission from the organization operating that installation. Use your own authorized account and follow the institution's security policies, applicable laws, and restrictions on handling personal or educational data. Do not attempt to bypass authentication, scope restrictions, Moodle capabilities, or approval requirements.

4. Operations and responsibility

Depending on configuration and authorization, MCP tools may read educational records or perform changes to courses, activities, users, enrolments, settings, and other Moodle data. Before approving a write operation, review its proposed effects and verify that you have appropriate authority. Users and organizations operating connected systems remain responsible for deciding which actions to authorize, maintaining backups, and verifying the outcome of material changes. Automated responses can be mistaken or incomplete.
