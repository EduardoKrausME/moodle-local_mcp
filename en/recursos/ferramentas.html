Explore and change Moodle data safely

Use ChatGPT or Claude to review courses, grades and participants or request changes in Moodle, always within your account permissions.

How it works

Ask which courses you can access, review activities and grades, check site health or find participants without navigating multiple screens.

To create a course, enrol users, update settings or make another permitted change, describe your request in the conversation. The AI prepares the operation using the features available to your account.

Before a protected operation is performed, Moodle provides a preview for your approval. Reading information and changing it are different actions, and Moodle's permissions always apply.

AI cannot execute changes that your Moodle account is not allowed to perform, and a preview alone does not modify data.
