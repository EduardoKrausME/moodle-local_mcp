Kopere BI + Moodle MCP

Explore Kopere BI reports in ChatGPT or Claude using Moodle tools exposed through the MCP protocol.

With Kopere BI and Moodle MCP installed, ask ChatGPT or Claude to find reports, compare courses and analyze enrolments, engagement and grades without manually exporting spreadsheets.

How to use it

  1. Install and configure Kopere BI and Moodle MCP on the same site.
  2. Connect Moodle to ChatGPT or Claude by following the Connections guide.
  3. Refresh the AI connection so it recognizes the reports your account can access.
  4. Ask it to list reports, choose one, filter results by course or period, and provide an analysis.

Try asking

“List my available Kopere BI reports, find those about engagement and performance, and compare the selected course results in charts and tables.”
“Read the enrolment report and summarize trends that could explain declining participation.”

What comes back

ChatGPT or Claude can prepare tables, indicators, comparisons and charts using results from reports that your Moodle account is authorized to access.

Users can only query reports they are allowed to access. Filtering another user or a cohort requires BI management permissions, and reports without a unique valid refkey are not exposed.
