Kopere Proctoring + Moodle MCP

Review Kopere Proctoring attempts, events and settings in ChatGPT or Claude through MCP, preserving permissions and human review.

With Kopere Proctoring and Moodle MCP installed, ask ChatGPT or Claude to check quiz monitoring, review attempts and organize recorded events for human assessment.

How to use it

  1. Install and configure Kopere Proctoring and Moodle MCP on the same site.
  2. Connect Moodle to ChatGPT or Claude with permission to read monitoring information. Only quiz managers should be allowed to request changes.
  3. Identify the quiz or attempt you want to review, then ask for a summary of events and recorded activity.
  4. To enable or disable monitoring, request the change and review the preview before approving it.

Try asking

“Check whether monitoring is enabled for quiz 456, and summarize the recorded attempts and event counts.”
“Build a timeline of monitoring events for attempt 789 in quiz 456, highlighting items that need human review rather than concluding misconduct.”

Available operations

Review monitoring settings, quiz attempts and recorded events. Authorized users can also request enabling or disabling monitoring with confirmation, while preserving plugin rules.

Monitoring events are observations, not automatic evidence of misconduct. Interpret them with institutional policies, privacy requirements and human review.
