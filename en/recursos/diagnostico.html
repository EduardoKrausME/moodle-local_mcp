Find issues and review operations

Use ChatGPT or Claude to check Moodle health, investigate problems and review operation records according to your account permissions.

How it works

Ask the AI to check site health, review relevant settings or identify performance and security concerns. Understand the situation before requesting changes.

Audit records help you review operations and confirmations, making it easier to investigate errors and follow up on actions performed through the integration.

If new plugin features do not appear in ChatGPT or Claude, check that the plugin is installed and enabled, then refresh the Moodle connection. Consult the technical guide for server or authentication issues.

Before sharing diagnostic information with third parties, review the records and remove passwords, credentials and unnecessary personal data.
