Helpdesk + Moodle MCP

Review Helpdesk tickets and knowledge articles in ChatGPT or Claude through MCP, with confirmation before changes.

With Helpdesk integrated into Moodle MCP, you can review tickets, read previous replies and find help articles directly in a conversation. Authorized users can also prepare replies and request changes with confirmation.

How to use it

  1. Install and configure Helpdesk and Moodle MCP on the same site.
  2. Connect Moodle to ChatGPT or Claude and authorize only the operations you need.
  3. Refresh the connection and ask it to list tickets, prioritize urgent requests or search the knowledge base.
  4. Request a draft reply or ticket update, then review the preview before approving the action.

Try asking

“List urgent open Helpdesk tickets and group them by priority so we can decide what to address first.”
“Read ticket 123 and find related knowledge-base guidance. Draft a response but send it only after I approve.”

Available operations

Review tickets, replies, categories, knowledge articles and support statistics. Authorized users can also create or update tickets, respond to requests and publish articles with confirmation.

Regular users can only see their own tickets. Support-wide queries, ticket administration and article creation require additional Moodle capabilities. Ticket creation and replies may trigger normal email notifications.
