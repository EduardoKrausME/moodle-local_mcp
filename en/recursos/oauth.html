Connect your account securely

Authorize ChatGPT or Claude to use Moodle through a secure connection, signing in to Moodle and keeping access within your account permissions.

How it works

Install and configure Moodle MCP, then use the connection guide to add Moodle to a compatible AI application. The site must be available over HTTPS, with authentication configured by the administrator.

When ChatGPT or Claude requests access, sign in to Moodle and review the authorization. The application can use only the features permitted by your Moodle account and the connection you have approved.

Review authorized connections and revoke access whenever needed. Administrative actions remain subject to Moodle permissions and confirmation before data is changed.

If the connection fails, the site administrator may need to review server and authentication settings. Technical OAuth details are covered in the dedicated guide.
