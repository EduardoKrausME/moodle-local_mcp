Connect ChatGPT

Connect Moodle to ChatGPT and explore courses, activities and participants in a conversation. This guide explains installation, connection and secure authorization.

How it works

After installing Moodle MCP, open the Connections page in Moodle administration. It provides a visual guide and the information needed to connect your Moodle site to ChatGPT.

In ChatGPT, add your Moodle connection using the details shown in Moodle. You do not need to write code or register individual tools.

When ChatGPT requests authorization, sign in with your Moodle account and review the access requested. Operations stay within your permissions and changes require approval.

Connect in five steps

  1. Install the plugin. Install Moodle MCP, complete the site update and confirm the Moodle site has a secure HTTPS address.
  2. Open Connections. In Moodle MCP administration, open Connections and copy the address provided to link your site to ChatGPT.
  3. Add it to ChatGPT. On chatgpt.com, open Plugins and add a custom MCP server using the name and URL shown by Moodle. Follow the authentication instructions on the Moodle Connections page.
  4. Authorize your account. Review the access request and sign in to Moodle. ChatGPT can only perform operations available to your account and the connection you approved.
  5. Test and refresh. In a conversation, ask: “List the courses I can view in this Moodle.” When you install more compatible plugins, refresh the connection to load their features.

For server administrators

If the connection fails even after following the guide, the Moodle web server may need additional configuration to enable secure authorization. Your infrastructure administrator can follow the technical authentication and web-server guide.

Common issues

  • Cannot connect: check that Moodle is reachable over HTTPS and follow the Connections page instructions.
  • Cannot authorize access: make sure your Moodle account is active and the administrator has enabled the required connection settings.
  • Missing courses or actions: check your account permissions in Moodle.
  • New plugin features are missing: enable the compatible plugin and refresh the ChatGPT connection.
ChatGPT does not receive permissions beyond those of your Moodle account. Operations that change data still need your approval.
