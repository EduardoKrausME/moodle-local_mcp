Manage courses and activities in a conversation

Ask ChatGPT or Claude to organize courses, create activities and prepare questions in Moodle, with previews and approval before changes.

How it works

Describe how you want to organize courses and categories, including requests to create multiple courses. The AI can prepare the structure and show what will be created before taking action.

Request pages, forums, books, quizzes, glossaries, H5P and other learning content when the corresponding features are available in your Moodle installation.

For assessments, ask to find question banks, organize categories and prepare questions. Access depends on user permissions, and changes require confirmation.

Images and files must be available for Moodle to receive them; provide an accessible file or link when required and follow the limits shown by the tool.
