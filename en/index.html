An integration that respects permissions

Fewer isolated integrations.
More useful context.

Use ChatGPT or Claude to access Moodle information and tools in a conversation. The plugin uses the MCP protocol and respects each user's Moodle permissions, without exposing information they cannot access through Moodle.

◎

Context-aware reading

Ask the AI to find courses, check grades, identify participants or review activities, quizzes and reports.

＋

Controlled changes

Request new content, enrolment changes or other updates. The AI shows you a preview and asks for approval before applying changes.

▤

Extensible integration

Install compatible Moodle plugins to extend what ChatGPT or Claude can access and manage, without writing code.

Your account, your permissions

A secure connection
with consent.

ChatGPT or Claude asks to access your Moodle site, but you decide whether to authorize the connection. Every request remains limited to what your Moodle account can access.

You can revoke access at any time, and operations that change data still need your explicit approval.

Viewing and changing information

Reading is one thing.
Changing is another.

Reading information and changing it are separate operations. The AI can find information and propose updates, but it only performs actions allowed by your Moodle account.

Before anything is created, edited or deleted, you can review exactly what would change and approve the action.

Two-step confirmation

AI proposes.
You approve.

Before making a change, ChatGPT or Claude shows you what it plans to do, and Moodle checks whether your account has permission for that action.

The change only runs after your explicit approval, and its result is recorded for auditing.

Security by design

Permissions, traceability
and practical limits.

ChatGPT or Claude cannot access more than your Moodle account allows, and you can control or revoke connections you have approved.

✓

Moodle capabilities

The AI respects your Moodle account permissions and course restrictions.

⌁

Protected credentials

Authentication details are protected and not displayed in conversations.

◉

Connection revocation

You can disconnect an authorized application and prevent it from making new requests.

▤

Audit without secrets

Moodle records completed operations without storing passwords or full conversation contents.
